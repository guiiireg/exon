Who am I ?

I'm Exon, a young computer science student who's passionate about coding.

I'm also a streamer on YouTube where I code multiple projects, it can be web, game or system development, it's totally random ahah.

Why YouTube

For months, I saw a lot of people use AI to code everything (myself included), but I realized that if I don't have AI, I can't do shit. Nothing left of my brain except Claude or ChatGPT... The code which I had so much fun inside is nothing more than a dream for me now.

I really wanted to code with no AI, but I couldn't. But that was before finding another student from japan, which was making me happy again, I saw her code without AI, and gave me hope in my future job and dream.

I started YouTube to force me to not use AI at all. Knowing that people are watching me, why should I use AI when I say it's bad ? And after a few months, I got the joy of my life, seeing me having issues on the easiest languages, I had tears ahah.
